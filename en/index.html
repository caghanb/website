Çağhan Benli

I am a specialist in plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery at Çanakkale Mehmet Akif Ersoy State Hospital, with a focus on microsurgery.

See appointment details

About

I studied medicine at Koç University and trained in plastic surgery at Çanakkale Onsekiz Mart University. I spent a period at Asan Medical Center in Seoul as a visiting scholar, and I hold the European Board (FEBOPRAS) and the Turkish board certification.

+See the career path
Clinic+Reconstructive microsurgery, aesthetic surgery, head, neck and facial surgery. Science+7 peer-reviewed articles, 11 congress presentations.

Three clinical areas

My practice concentrates on three areas of plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery: microsurgical repair of tissue loss, aesthetic surgery, and surgery of the head, neck and face.

Questions

+
How do I book an appointment?+

Outpatient appointments are booked through MHRS, the national appointment system, or by calling 182.

Which hospital do you work at?+

Since October 2026 I have worked at the Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgery Outpatient Clinic of Çanakkale Mehmet Akif Ersoy State Hospital.

Can I ask a medical question by email?+

Medical assessments cannot be made by email; diagnosis and treatment decisions are made after an examination. In an emergency, call 112.

How can I reach you about academic collaboration?+

Write to drcaghanbenli@gmail.com. My publications are listed on my Google Scholar, ORCID and Scopus profiles.

Which languages do you speak?+

Turkish (native), English (C1) and basic Italian (A2).
